How to Play:

  • 4 players required
  • One player is the Imposter
  • Others are Detectives
  • Detectives see the word, Imposter sees only a hint
  • Each player writes a word related to the secret word (40 seconds)
  • There will be 3 rounds after which you will vote who the Imposter is
  • Detectives win if they eliminate the Imposter
  • Imposter wins if they survive all rounds
